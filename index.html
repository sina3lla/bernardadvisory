02 / Beneath the surface

“It’s probably
nothing.”

Sometimes, that’s where we start.

The conversation ends.
The feeling doesn’t.

You agreed to something you didn’t want. A familiar pattern shows up again. You know there is more to the situation, but you can’t quite name it.

My work starts there: separating what happened from what you assumed, noticing the dynamic, and considering what you can do next.

Notice.Question.Choose.

Read about it in the books. Explore it with the companion. Bring your own situation into a conversation.
